The Organizational Insight Brief

Not a report.
A diagnosis.

Some organizations look at their data and know exactly what to do. Others want us to tell them what it means.

The Organizational Insight Brief does exactly that. We talk to your leaders. We pulse your team. Then we tell you what is actually happening, why, and where to start.

The excerpt to the right is from a real client engagement. The organization's name has been removed.

Organizational Insight Brief
Sample · Confidential
What's really going on

You don't have a collaboration problem. You have a scoreboard problem. Your team is being asked to collaborate, but the system they're measured against still rewards individual wins. In a high-pressure environment, protective behavior isn't resistance. It's a rational response to the design.

Perception vs. reality

Leadership believes the team is aligned and motivated. What the data shows is a Fairness & Recognition score of 38, the lowest of the six conditions, and open text that consistently describes effort going unacknowledged.

Where to start

Fairness & Recognition is your starting point. Not because it's easiest to fix, but because it's the most predictive leading indicator of voluntary turnover in your environment.
