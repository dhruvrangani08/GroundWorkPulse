Qualitative layer

Words, not just numbers.

Every pulse includes three open-text prompts. Numbers tell you where. Words tell you why. These are the prompts we ask every team, every cycle.

Prompt 01
What is something leadership may not fully understand about what it feels like to work here right now?
Prompt 02
What most helps your team do great work, and what most gets in the way?
Prompt 03
If one thing changed in the next 90 days, what would make the biggest difference?
Methodology

How we measure.

01 · Scoring

Zero to 100, per condition.

Each condition scores 0 to 100. The lowest is automatically flagged as your focus for the cycle.

02 · Anonymity

Completely anonymous.

Responses are never tied to individuals.

03 · Cadence

Quarterly pulses.

Quarterly pulses show what's actually improving over time.

04 · Sample size

We won't report a score we don't trust.

A minimum number of responses is required before a score is shown. Otherwise, it's not real data.

Origin

How the framework came together.

The framework is built from two sources: decades of research on how teams perform, and direct experience working inside them.

Across industries and environments, the same pattern emerges. When these conditions are strong, people perform and stay. When they break down, everything else follows.

This isn't theory. It's a system built from how real teams actually operate.
